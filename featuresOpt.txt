MYFLIPPERGAME v1.3.0 - FEATURES & OPTIONS REFERENCE
=====================================================

FILES
-----
index.html    - page structure, canvas, controls, panels
style.css     - neon theme, layout, responsive styles
game.js       - complete game (IIFE, ~1500 lines, 22 commented sections)
regression.js - headless test harness (node regression.js -> 100 checks)

RUN
---
Open index.html in any modern browser, or serve the folder:
  npx serve .        /     python -m http.server
No build step, no dependencies.

CONTROLS
--------
Keyboard
  Left flipper ......... ArrowLeft  or  A
  Right flipper ........ ArrowRight or  D
  Start game ........... Space (also W or ArrowUp)
  Plunger .............. HOLD Space / W / ArrowUp to charge the spring,
                          release to launch (power oscillates 0-100%)
  Tilt (cheat warning) . hold Left + Right together

Pointer / touch
  Left / Right flipper buttons (bottom of page)
  PLUNGER button (center, yellow) - hold to charge, release to launch
  All buttons use Pointer Events with pointer capture; safe on touch.

Safety
  Window blur releases flippers and cancels plunger charge.

GAMEPLAY
--------
Plunger lane (right side)
  New ball always waits on the plunger. Hold the trigger to charge the
  spring (oscillating meter on the right edge), release to fire.
  Launch speed scales with charge (710-1000 px/s). The lane is capped by
  a 45-degree deflector that throws the ball left into the playfield, and
  guarded by a one-way gate: balls can leave the lane into the field but
  cannot fall back in from the field. A weak shot that does not clear the
  lane rolls back onto the plunger for another try.

Scoring
  Bumpers ...... 100 / 150 / 200 / 250 (color coded, outer ring first)
  Spinners ..... 30 per touch, spin up with ball speed; the ball
                  bounces off the spinner post (normal velocity is
                  reflected, so it can never get stuck on it)
  Ramps ........ 100 per pass
  Drop targets . 50 each, 5 in one ball = PERFECT BALL bonus + level up
  Flipper hits . 10 (only while the flipper is being flipped)
  Combo ........ consecutive hits within 2.2 s chain the multiplier up to
                  x10 (HUD badge shows up to x3)

Flippers
  Pressing a flipper kicks the ball hard: up to 390 px/s upward
  (Normal) plus a sideways push - about 50% higher than v1.2.2.
  A resting flipper is just a slope: balls touching it bounce off
  gently (segment reflection only) and roll toward the drain,
  like a real table. Only a pressed flipper launches the ball.
  Flipper gap widened (pivots at x=90 / x=270) for reliable drain.

Multiball
  Hitting a 250-point bumper has a 15% chance to trigger CHAOS multiball.

Lives / drain
  Ball drains between the flippers. Difficulty-dependent balls:
  Easy=5, Normal=4, Hard=3.
  After each drain the next ball waits on the plunger.

Tilt
  Holding both flippers: haptic warning after ~0.5 s ([100,50,100] pulse),
  TILT - GAME OVER after ~1.5 s ([200,100,200] pulse). Tilt ends the game
  immediately and works even while the ball is on the plunger.

TARGET BANK (Decay Mechanic)
  5 drop targets at top of playfield. Each hit lights for 25 seconds,
  then decays (pulses when <5s remaining) and becomes hittable again.
  Level advances when ALL 5 are lit SIMULTANEOUSLY - classic pinball bank
  completion. PERFECT BALL bonus (15k) = hit all 5 unique in one ball.
  HUD shows "TARGETS: X/5" with yellow when complete, cyan when partial.

LEVELS (12, selectable from Settings -> Levels)
  1 CLASSIC    2 HIGH & LOW   3 GRID      4 DIAMOND
  5 TWIN TOWERS 6 SWIRL       7 BOWL      8 CROSSFIRE
  9 FORTRESS   10 HOURGLASS   11 STORM    12 FINAL
  The level panel renders a live miniature of every table (3x4 grid);
  click a table to start that level immediately. Clearing the target bank
  (all 5 lit at once) advances to the next level automatically.
  Level layouts adjusted: all items leave 20px margin from plunger lane
  divider at x=306 so ball cannot squeeze through gaps.
  Level label (LV n) and version (v1.3.0) drawn on playfield bottom-left.

MISSIONS (Settings -> Missions, progress bars + rewards)
  TRIPLE THREAT . chain a x3 combo ........................ +2,500
  50K CLUB ...... reach 50,000 points ..................... +10,000
  SURVIVOR ...... play 60 s in one game ................... +5,000
  TARGET PRACTICE hit 15 drop targets .................... +3,000
  CHAOS ......... trigger multiball ....................... +5,000
  PERFECT BALL .. clear all 5 targets in one ball ......... +15,000

OPTIONS (Settings panel, all persist to localStorage)
  Sound ....... ON/OFF (WebAudio synth: bumpers, flippers, drain, jingles)
  Vibration ... ON/OFF (navigator.vibrate haptics on mobile)
  Difficulty .. Easy / Normal / Hard
                 Easy:   gravity 300, ball speed x0.85, flipper x1.15,
                         bumper x1.2, 5 balls
                 Normal: gravity 360, ball speed x1.00, flipper x1.00,
                         bumper x1.0, 4 balls
                 Hard:   gravity 420, ball speed x1.15, flipper x0.90,
                         bumper x0.85, 3 balls
  High Scores . top 10 scores with dates (view from Settings)
  Levels ...... level select grid (see above)

PERSISTENCE KEYS (localStorage)
  myFlipperScores    - JSON array of {score, date}, top 10
  myFlipperBest      - best score number
  myFlipperSound     - "true"/"false"
  myFlipperVibrate   - "true"/"false"
  myFlipperDifficulty- "easy"/"normal"/"hard"

v1.3.0 CHANGES (vs v1.2.0)
  + Flipper gap widened: pivots moved to x=90 / x=270 (was 100/250)
    giving ~46px tip gap (was 26px) for reliable mobile drain
  + Difficulty lives rebalanced: Easy=5, Normal=4, Hard=3 (was 12/9/6)
  + Level layouts adjusted: all right-side items shifted left to leave
    20px margin from plunger lane divider (x=306), fixing ball-squeeze
    bugs where ball could pass through gaps near right wall
  + Version updated to v1.3.0 on playfield

v1.6.0 CHANGES (vs v1.5.0)
  + Target bank centered: all 12 levels now have 5 drop targets centered on
    playfield center x=165 (was offset in LV 1 & 3)
  + Edge margins enforced: all ramps, spinners, bumpers keep 20px+ clearance
    from left wall (x=24) and plunger divider (x=306)
  + Fixed slope bars connected to flipper pivots: bottom guide slopes now
    meet flipper bases at (101,510) and (259,510), eliminating ball-squeeze
    gaps where ball could drain between slope and flipper
  + Version updated to v1.6.0 on playfield

v1.2.0 CHANGES (vs v1.1.0)
  + Target bank decay mechanic (25s timer, simultaneous lit = level advance)
  + Target visual states: lit (neon glow + timer bar), unhit (normal)
  + Right flipper moved inboard (260->250) for better drain gap
  + Flipper collision radius increased (7->10) + checked before walls
  + Level advance = all 5 targets lit at once (authentic pinball)
  + PERFECT BALL = 5 unique hits per ball (separate 15k achievement)
  + Version label on playfield (bottom-left)
  + Right flipper edge gap fixed
